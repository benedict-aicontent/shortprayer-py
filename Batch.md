## DAY 1 — PEACE OVER THE ONES YOU LOVE

**EMOTIONAL HOOK**

Pray for the ones you can't carry.

**PRAYER**

Father God, I lift up the people I love, every name that is on my heart today. You know their worries, their struggles, and the things they do not say out loud. Wrap Your peace around them like a steady hand. Guard their hearts, calm their fears, and draw them close to You. Help me trust that they are safe in Your care, even when I cannot fix things. In Jesus' name, Amen

**YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION**
